AgriPass issues an attendance certificate to each attendee who meets the event's minimum session attendance requirement. Each certificate carries both PAA signatories and a QR code for verification.

Who qualifies

An attendee is eligible when they attend at least the event's minimum attendance percentage of sessions (80% by default).

The event's Certificates tab shows each registrant’s eligibility and attendance requirement.

Before issuing

  1. Check the settings on the Certificates tab: attendance requirement and both signatories. Use Edit to change them.
  2. Upload both signatories' signatures under Signature & layout. A PNG with a transparent background looks best, up to 2 MB. Without one, a blank line is printed for signing by hand.
  3. Click Preview sample certificate to check the layout.
  4. Mark the event as Completed so attendance is final. Certificates cannot be issued before this.

Issuing

  1. Click Issue & email. AgriPass creates a certificate for every eligible attendee who does not have one yet, and emails it to them. Large events are processed in batches automatically; keep the page open until it finishes.
  2. To issue to one person, for example after correcting their attendance, use Issue on their row.
  3. If an attendee did not receive the email, use the envelope button (Email again) on their row. Attendees can also download their certificate any time from My AgriPass or from their pass page.

Certificates show the name as it was when issued. If a member's details are wrong, correct them on the Members page before issuing.

Revoking

If a certificate was issued in error, use the red Revoke button on its row and enter a reason. The certificate stays on record for audit, but verification shows it as revoked and the attendee can no longer download it.

Verification

Anyone, including an employer, can scan the certificate's QR code or enter its code at {{APP_URL}}/verify/ to confirm it is genuine and still valid.
