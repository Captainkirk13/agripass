The event's Registrations tab lists everyone who registered, with their chapter, PRC license, answers to your questions and how many sessions they attended. Use the search box to find someone by name, email, PRC number or chapter. Click a name to open their member record.

Reviewing registrations

Search registrants by name, email, PRC license or chapter. Open a member record to review their profile, attendance and certificates. Refresh offline scanner rosters to include new registrations.

Passes

Each registration gets one QR pass, emailed to the attendee and shown on screen after registering. The pass link works on any phone and can be printed. If someone registers twice with the same email, the second attempt is refused: one person, one pass per event.
